# Claim 2 source audit

Definitions 2.1–2.3 define `V(N)=D(N)b(N)` and sufficient volume as
`Omega(log N)`. The paragraph after Theorem 4.2 interprets the theorem: at
constant precision the condition forces log width, while at log precision even
constant width supplies log volume.

That interpretation retains polynomial padding, polynomial maximum width,
uniformity, fixed-point semantics, and the precision regimes. The paper says
insufficient-volume padded models remain difficult to characterize.
